HOTELS IN TAUNGGYI

Taunggyi is the capital and largest city of Shan State, Myanmar and lies on the Thazi-Kyaingtong road at an elevation of 4,712 feet, just north of Shwenyaung and Inle Lake within the Myelat region. Taunggyi is the fifth largest city of Myanmar, and has an estimated population of 380,665 as of 2014
The district is the home to many attractive tourist spots of the country that are varied from natural, cultural, to historical sites. The district's capital itself, Taunggyi City is famous for the Hot air balloon festival which is held annually in the eighth month of traditional Burmese Calendar.
