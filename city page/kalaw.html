HOTELS IN KALAW

The town of Kalaw is set amongst magnificent pine forests and the area has become very popular with trekkers. Pleasantly cool all the year round, this sylvan offers the best hill trekking views among the British colonial landscape. Kalaw is a hill town in the Shan State of Myanmar. Located in Kalaw Township in Tayungyi District, it would be a super duper dope experience if you want to spend time going around enjoying the feel of being authentic Burmese. With tons of activities and marketplaces around, Kalaw really worth exploring.
